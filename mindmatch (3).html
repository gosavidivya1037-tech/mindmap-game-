How to Play

Master MindMatch in four simple steps.

1

Tap any face-down card to flip it and reveal its symbol.

2

Tap a second card. If the symbols match, both stay face-up and count toward your matches.

3

If they don't match, both flip back down after a short pause — memorize their positions for next time.

4

Clear the whole board to finish. Fewer moves, faster time, and higher accuracy mean a better score.

Scoring

Your final score rewards speed, efficiency, and precision — scaled up for harder difficulties. Fewer wasted flips and a quicker clear both push your score higher.

Overview

MindMatch is a polished memory-matching game focused on concentration, pattern recognition, and competitive scoring — built to demonstrate both game-development ability and product-level UI/UX design.

Key Features

🔀 Dynamic card generation

✨ Professional game UI
🔊 Sound effects
📈 Progression system

Development Highlights

Game state management, deterministic shuffle & matching logic, an animation system built on CSS 3D transforms, a difficulty-scaled scoring model, session-based persistence, and a fully responsive layout system.
